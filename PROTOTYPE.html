How NeuroD Works

Three kinds of AI.
One goal.

Here's what's actually happening behind the scenes.

Pattern Recognition AI
Powers "Early Signals"

Every check-in feeds into pattern detection — trend analysis across 7, 14, or 30 days. It spots your sleep drifting or social contact dropping before you're consciously aware.

✓ Runs on your device · Works offline · Data never leaves your phone
Causal Analysis AI
Powers "Root Causes"

Most apps tell you what happened. NeuroD tells you why. It analyses temporal sequences — what changes first, what follows, with what delay — to find genuine causes, not just correlations.

Days 1–14: Building baseline
Days 14–30: Correlations found
Days 30–60: Early causal links
Days 60+: Confident personal causal model
✓ Progressive · Honest · No fake insights to fill a screen
Generative AI
Powers "Your Plan" & Chat

Translates signals and causes into a plan you can act on — in your language, at your energy level, for your brain type. Adapts to your neuro profile, strengths, and reading preference.

✓ Every output labelled AI-generated · Not a therapist
Powered by Claude (Anthropic). Data never used for AI training.
✦

Together, these three do something none can do alone.

Pattern recognition spots the signal. Causal analysis finds the lever. Generative AI puts it in your hands.

About NeuroD
For the 1 in 8

Mental illness affects nearly a billion people.

Most will never receive adequate care. For neurodiverse individuals, the gap is wider still.

NeuroD exists to close that gap. Not to replace professional care, but to be the companion that watches your patterns when no one else can, finds the real causes behind hard days, and builds a plan that works with your brain.

Our principles
1. Strengths first, always

Every plan starts with what you're good at.

2. Honest AI

Not enough data? We say so. No fake insights to fill a screen.

3. The 3am test

Every feature works when you have almost nothing left to give.

4. Your data is yours

Encrypted. Never sold. Delete with one button.

5. No shame mechanics

No streaks. No badges. No guilt. NeuroD is here when you come back.

Full details

What data we collect
›

Profile info you enter. Journal check-ins. Usage patterns (capacity inference only). We don't collect: location, contacts, photos, or microphone audio.

Where your data lives
›

Device: IndexedDB (offline). Cloud: Supabase with row-level security. AI: Anthropic API, encrypted, not retained.

Who can see your data
›

You. That's it. Engineers can't access individual data. No third parties, analytics companies, or advertisers.

Your GDPR rights
›

Help & FAQ
Getting started
Do I need a diagnosis?
›

No. "Not sure yet" and "Just exploring" give you full access from day one.

How often should I check in?
›

Whenever feels right. Daily is best for insights, but no penalty for gaps. No streaks. No guilt.

Insights
Why "building your baseline"?
›

NeuroD needs 14+ check-ins to spot patterns. We'd rather show nothing than something unreliable.

How is this different from a mood tracker?
›

Mood trackers show what happened. NeuroD shows why — and where to intervene. "Your mood is low" vs "sensory overload 2 days ago caused this."

Your plan
Who writes my care plan?
›

Claude (Anthropic), given your profile, strengths, check-ins, and patterns. Every output labelled AI-generated.

Is this medical advice?
›

No. NeuroD is a support tool. Always talk to your GP about care changes.

Why only one weekly focus?
›

Multiple priorities overwhelm executive function. One thing done is worth three things planned.

Technical
Does it work offline?
›

Yes. Check-ins save locally and sync when you're back online.

Can I install it on my phone?
›

Yes — "Add to Home Screen" from your browser. Works like a native app, no store needed.
